Frequently asked questions

Can I submit an unofficial transcript?

No. Applicants must upload an official transcript sent or sealed by their school. Unofficial copies are not accepted.

Who can write my recommendation letters?

One letter must come from a teacher who taught you in grade 11 or 12. The second may come from a coach, employer or community leader. Letters from family members are not accepted.

Is there an interview?

Finalists are invited to a 20-minute video interview. Interviews are by invitation only.

Can I use the award at a community college?

Yes. The award can be used at any accredited two-year or four-year college in the United States.

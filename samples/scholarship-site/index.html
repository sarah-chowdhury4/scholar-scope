Riverbend Future Leaders Scholarship

A $5,000 award for students who have led a project that improved their school or community.

Important dates

Applications openSeptember 1, 2026
Application deadlineJanuary 15, 2027 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time (ET)
Recommendation letters dueJanuary 22, 2027 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time (ET)
Finalist interviewsMarch 3, 2027
Award notificationApril 10, 2027

What to submit

  • Online application form.
  • Personal essay of no more than 650 words: describe a project you led and what changed because of it.
  • Official high school transcript.
  • Two letters of recommendation, at least one from a teacher.
  • A resume listing activities, work and volunteer experience (one page).

Read the eligibility requirements before you apply. Answers to common questions are in the FAQ.

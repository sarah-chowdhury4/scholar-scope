Eligibility requirements

Riverbend Future Leaders Scholarship

To be eligible, an applicant must:

  • Be a high school senior graduating in 2027.
  • Have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale.
  • Plan to enroll full-time at an accredited two-year or four-year college in fall 2027.
  • Be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident.
  • Show financial need, documented by a Student Aid Index (SAI) of 15,000 or less.

Not eligible

  • Children of Riverbend Community Foundation staff or board members.
  • Students who already hold a bachelor's degree.
